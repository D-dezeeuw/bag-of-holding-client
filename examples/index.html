Bag of Holding

Table software for a game that remembers.

The World Atlas

Look at a generated world: continents and climates, sea lanes and waygates, the powers and their wars, the crowns and who wears them, and the floor plan of a single place. Toggle between the GM's spoiler view and the player's — where undiscovered ground is deleted from the data, not dimmed.

Initiative Tracker

Roll a combat order and run the round: turn advance, conditions, concentration and death saves, kept honest by the engine rather than by whoever is holding the pen.
Every page here is plain ES modules over static files — no build step, no dependencies, no key. To render a real campaign instead of a rolled one, a host feeds these components a baked cartridge or the MCP server's world_atlas payload; see docs/world-atlas.md.
